All three final source pages were opened, not merely inferred from search snippets. Each question has exactly three parallel options and one intended correct answer. Correct positions are balanced B/C/A. Explanations are below the source project's 32-word ceiling, though this untimed website is a distinct format.

The two brand field notes express Quiznauts editorial intent and mascot-design decisions. They make no measured learning-outcome, subscriber, pass-rate, schedule, or audience-size claims. "Come for the cow. Leave knowing more." is working editorial copy, not a claim about trademark approval or demonstrated educational outcomes.

The final Venus citation uses NASA Space Place, which explicitly distinguishes 243-day rotation from a sunrise every 117 days. The main NASA Venus Facts page was also read but has an inconsistent sunrise-to-sunset phrase; it is not used to substantiate the solar-day detail. [NASA Mars Facts](https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/) and [NASA Earth Facts](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/facts/) were also opened for the two distractor planets; both rotate far faster than they orbit.
