# Quiznauts launchpad

Created October 22, 2026 at Ryan's request: a Hugo companion site plus a practical marketing and visual-asset scaffold for the Quiznauts channel.

The personal outcome is one local place to prepare the public presence, keep copy and assets consistent, and resume without reconstructing decisions. Hugo is the only site build dependency. Python standard-library helpers provide short commands, local checks and downloadable kits. This authoring workflow remains local and single-user even when its output is eventually public.

The visitor outcome is simple: meet the adorable Mr. Big, try a short quiz, learn something useful, read field notes and find verified Quiznauts destinations when connected. The toy cow is approachable; the editorial work supplies the substance. The audience is curious teens and adults, with a warm, witty, respectful voice.

The current visual direction follows the sibling Quiznauts player’s October 22 cut-paper revision: navy night sky, cream banners, gold stars, vermilion terrain, colored answer coins and condensed system display type. The homepage, navigation and mission art use its printed Mr. Big and wordmark. The earlier v1.4 production library and marketing exports are preserved separately; `site-style-source.json` records the four copied public player images. Rounded toy anatomy, photo-based markings and a clean helmet collar remain consistent. Information text is clean and high contrast; texture is decoration.

This first build includes a homepage, a playable three-question sample with sourced explanations and a no-JavaScript reveal path, mission and field-note collections, about/links/site-notes pages, a downloadable media kit, platform artwork, profile copy and a proposed launch sequence. The sequence is a plan, not an automation or a promised publishing schedule.

No channel URL, owned handle or public domain was found in the supplied project. `data/properties.json` holds empty destinations until verified; `hugo.toml` defaults to loopback preview and no indexing. There is no signup form, contact backend, analytics, login, advertising service or account creation. Publishing and external setup remain owner-controlled actions.

Local build/source checks establish what the files contain. Browser layout, actual zoom, keyboard/screen-reader use and platform upload/crop acceptance are separate checks. Their status belongs in the handoff record, not in unsupported conformance claims.
