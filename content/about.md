Quiznauts is a place to play with what you know and leave with something new. This first website mission explores the ocean, a planet, and an animal. Future questions can travel across science, history, geography, culture, technology, and the wonderfully odd.

Your companion is **Mr. Big**, a real toy cow reimagined as an astronaut. His tiny smile and round paws set the tone: welcoming, curious, and game for an expedition. Behind that friendly face, the editorial job is serious. Questions should be fair, sources should support the answers, and explanations should make your time worthwhile.

## How to play

Choose A, B, or C. Make a guess, read the explanation, and follow a source when something catches your attention. The website's sample mission is untimed and needs no account.

The planned channel format uses ten-question missions and three-question quick missions. This site begins with one playable three-question sample, deeper explanations, and the artwork that gives Quiznauts its character. Verified channel links will appear when they are ready.

## What you can expect

- Clear questions with one defensible answer.
- Encouragement without a baby voice or a lecture.
- Interesting explanations, including when your guess misses.
- Source links for the factual material on this site.
- A little 1950s space-program optimism, with readable modern controls.
