Mr. Big started as a toy cow. Becoming an astronaut seemed like a reasonable next step.

He is the face of Quiznauts: a small, soft-looking explorer with button eyes, round paws, and an impressively practical glass helmet. His black markings come from the actual toy, including the broad patch high on the right side of the picture. The forehead and the fur around both eyes stay white.

The art borrows its optimism from mid-century space advertising: cream paper, navy ink, gold stars, and angular vermilion landscapes. Think of an invitation to a very friendly science fair. The questions can travel further than the poster suggests.

That is the balance we want. Mr. Big makes the first step feel welcoming. The quizzes need to reward that step with clear questions, accurate answers, and explanations that add something useful. An adorable cow can introduce a demanding question without making a wrong answer feel like a personal failing.

He will never need fingers to point out a good fact. A whole round paw will do.
