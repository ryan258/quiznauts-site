## A small site by design

This site is generated with Hugo. Its pages, styles, scripts, and brand artwork are served as ordinary files. The quiz adds a small amount of JavaScript for answer selection and feedback; the questions and answer explanations are also available without it.

## Your answers

The sample mission runs in your browser. Answers are not submitted to Quiznauts, and the quiz does not create an account. Reloading the page resets the current attempt. This site includes no analytics script, newsletter signup, advertising code, or embedded social feed.

If the site is hosted publicly, the hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs. A hosting choice and its log policy must be checked before publication. Links to NASA, NOAA, the Monterey Bay Aquarium, or social platforms take you to other sites with their own practices.

## Reading and interaction

The design uses system fonts, strong reading contrast, visible focus styles, labelled answer choices, and controls intended to be comfortable to select. Feedback includes words as well as color. There is no timed question sequence and no automatically playing mascot animation.

Accessibility is ongoing work. These design choices do not establish complete WCAG conformance or replace keyboard, zoom, screen-reader, and human visual review. The local project includes focused verification instructions so those checks can be completed before launch.

## Sources and corrections

Factual quiz answers and the Venus field note link to their supporting sources. The current source check is dated October 22, 2026. Facts can need more context, and web pages can change; a source check records what was reviewed, rather than promising that a page will remain unchanged.
