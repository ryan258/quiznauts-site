## Keep the cow consistent

Use round paws and oval feet, button eyes, a tiny closed smile, and one clean helmet collar. Preserve the asymmetric black patches: a broad upper-temple patch on the viewer's right connects to that ear; the forehead and both eyes sit on white fur. Do not mirror the head to change a pose.

Keep essential text free of grain and decoration. Use ink or teal on cream, or cream on teal. Any question placed in a thumbnail or social graphic must also be in the linked mission or video.
