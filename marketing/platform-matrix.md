# Where each property earns its place

This is an editorial recommendation for a manageable launch, checked against the current project scope on October 22, 2026. It makes no claim about platform algorithms, audience size, account availability, or current upload limits.

## Launch recommendation

Give YouTube and the site clear jobs. Choose **one** secondary outlet after reviewing its actual setup and upload flow. Create a complete reusable package once, then adapt its crop and caption. The aim is a coherent trail from an interesting question to its answer and source.

Reserving or creating every account is not required to finish this launch scaffold. If a property adds repetitive work without helping Ryan share a quiz he already wants to make, leave its URL empty and retain the local art for later.

## A small audience-evidence notebook

After a real post exists, note its URL, topic, posting date, and any actual observations in `status.json` or a dated note. A viewer comment asking for clarification is useful evidence. So is a confusing crop. Do not invent targets, claim retention gains, or infer learning from a score.

If platform analytics are later used, record the property, exact reporting period, metric definition, and source of the number. Nothing in this starter records live analytics or makes success claims.
