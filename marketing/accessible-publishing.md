# Accessible publishing: the short review

Use on the final composition and real upload preview. This checklist is an editorial aid, not an accessibility-conformance certificate.

## Words and images

- Can the essential question and choices be read at the size people will actually see?
- Is information text on a clean solid panel, with ink/teal on cream or cream on teal?
- Does the caption include the meaningful text from the graphic, including A/B/C and the question?
- Are correctness and selected answers communicated in words or symbols as well as color?
- Does alt text explain the image's purpose without repeating an entire adjacent caption unnecessarily?
- Are Mr. Big's face and asymmetric patches intact after cropping? No mirrored head, fingers, human teeth, or stacked helmet rims.
- Does the hook match an actual question in the linked mission/video? No invented pass rates, fake urgency, or unsupported learning results.

## Motion and sound

- Use a static image for profile art and a still preview when possible.
- Review the complete real video at its intended size and pacing. A text-fit check alone cannot establish comfortable reading time.
- If there is speech, supply accurate speech-aligned captions and inspect names, answer letters and timing. A silent on-screen quiz and a narrated quiz have different caption needs.
- Do not add flashes or rapid contrast pulses. Keep essential text clear of grain, shake and decorative motion.
- Preserve the one-play or user-controlled intent when selecting a motion export. A platform may loop it differently; inspect the actual post behavior.

## Reusable alt text starters

**Avatar:** Mr. Big, a smiling toy cow astronaut with button eyes, a round glass helmet, and a black patch high on the viewer's right temple.

**Welcome composition:** Mr. Big waves a round teal paw beside the Quiznauts name on a cream and teal space-program poster.

**Question card:** Quiz question: Which is Earth's largest ocean basin? A, Atlantic. B, Pacific. C, Indian. The cream card includes a First Contact heading and NOAA source credit.

**Reveal card:** Answer: B, Pacific. It is Earth's largest and deepest ocean basin. A NOAA source is included in the caption.

Change the description when the actual art differs. Do not use an answer-reveal alt on a question-only card or describe a decorative cow when the meaningful information is the quiz question.

## Link check

Before posting, replace every bracketed destination with a verified public address. Test it from the final caption or profile field. A local `/missions/first-contact/` path needs a real public domain before it can work for social visitors.
