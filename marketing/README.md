# Quiznauts launch desk

Local launch kit, October 22, 2026. The site and artwork are concrete assets; account setup, publication, and the launch date still belong to Ryan. Nothing here schedules a post, creates an account, collects email, spends on ads, or promises a cadence.

The working proposition: an adorable little toy cow astronaut invites curious teens and adults into substantial quizzes. The question earns attention; the explanation earns trust.

## Resume here

1. Open `status.json`. It records what exists and the next owner decisions.
2. Use `profiles.md` for display names, measured bios, and the channel About draft.
3. Use `platform-matrix.md` to choose one optional secondary outlet.
4. Work through `launch-sequence.md` at your pace; its 14 slots are a proposed sequence, not a public daily commitment.
5. Choose a draft in `posts.md`, then use `one-session-workflow.md` to assemble a complete package.
6. Check `accessible-publishing.md` before any upload.

Ready art appears on the website's `/media/` page and in `data/marketing.json`. Those are editable/production assets. Captions, profile copy, handle candidates, and launch timing in this folder are **drafts**. A graphic being exported does not make the attached claim, destination, or post approved.

## The two useful destinations

**The channel:** the actual quiz videos, once an owned channel and reviewed upload are confirmed.

**The site:** a place to try a mission, read a useful explanation, and find the official properties. The current build is local; it has no verified public domain. Put the real destination in `data/properties.json` before publication. Keep unverified social URLs empty so the site does not advertise invented accounts.

## Keep the system small

Start with YouTube, this site, and one optional secondary social outlet. Reuse a reviewed mission rather than writing a separate stream for every platform. One good question can support a paired question/reveal graphic, a short clip if produced, and a field note. There is no newsletter backend, multi-user marketing platform, or paid campaign in this scaffold.
